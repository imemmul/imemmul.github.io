  • 2026 · ongoing

    Joint Uncertainty for Feed-Forward 3D Reconstruction

    Feed-forward reconstruction models such as VGGT and Pi3 give a confidence value for each point separately. Across five models and three datasets (ScanNet++, ARKitScenes, Waymo), I found that their errors are strongly correlated between nearby points, so pointwise confidence misjudges the uncertainty of measurements that involve several points. I am developing a model of these joint errors.

    3D reconstruction · uncertainty · VGGT · Pi3

  • 2024–2026

    MonoMIP: Multi-Height Inverse Perspective Mapping

    Monocular 3D object detection that projects image features onto several horizontal planes from the road surface to roof height, giving the detector a depth-dependent cue. Highest KITTI test AP3D (Car, Moderate) among published monocular methods without extra data, as of October 2026.
