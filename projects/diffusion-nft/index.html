Diffusion-Based Valuable NFT Generation

Emir Ulurak*, Beyza Kaya*, Emre Sefer

Ozyegin University · * equal contribution

PDFDOIBibTeX

Overview

My B.Sc. senior project, supervised by Emre Sefer and carried out with Beyza Kaya (equal contribution), published in Multimedia Tools and Applications. We fine-tune Stable Diffusion with reinforcement learning (PPO) to generate images with rare visual attributes while preserving prompt fidelity.

  • A rarity-aware reward from a ViT-B/16 rarity classifier, combined with ImageReward for visual quality.
  • KL regularisation towards the supervised fine-tuned model balances rarity against visual coherence.
  • Evaluation of rarity and visual quality, including reward-weight ablations.
